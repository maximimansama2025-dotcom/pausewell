# Pausewell

A gentle wellness app for short, mood-based resets.

**Live app:** https://pausewell.netlify.app/

Pausewell recommends a small ritual based on how you feel and how much time you have. Try guided breathing, grounding, gentle movement, a mindful walk, reflection, or a water break. Track your recent pauses without streak pressure.

Your mood preference and pause history are saved locally in your browser. No account or backend is required.

Pausewell is a general wellness companion, not a medical tool.
